Privacy Policy — Vintage Digital Clock

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Vintage Digital Clock ("the app") is developed by Arim Seventh.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The app requests no Android permissions, including internet access, so it cannot send any data off your device.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (selected color, whether seconds are shown, whether the colon blinks) are saved locally on your device so they are kept between sessions. This data never leaves your device, except through Android's own backup feature if you have enabled it in your system settings; such backups are handled by Google under your Google account and are not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes these preferences.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/arimeq/8-segment-clock/issues.
